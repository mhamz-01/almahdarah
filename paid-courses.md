Noorani Qaida
Master the Foundations of Quranic Reading
Noorani Qaida is the essential first step for anyone beginning their Quran learning journey. This course builds a strong foundation in Arabic letter recognition, correct pronunciation, and reading fluency — preparing students to read the Quran with confidence and accuracy.
Prerequisites
None — this course is designed for complete beginners. No prior Quran reading experience required.
What You'll Learn
•	Correct pronunciation of Arabic letters, both individually and in combined forms
•	Makharij (articulation points) — learning exactly where and how each letter is pronounced from the mouth and throat
•	Reading practice in both Uthmani and Indo-Pak script, so students become comfortable with whichever mushaf they'll use going forward
•	Basic Tajweed rules to prepare for advanced Quran reading
Course Duration
2 to 4 months, based on the student's pace and progress
Assessment
Regular tests and assessments throughout the course to track progress and ensure mastery before moving to the next level
Certification
Students receive a certificate upon successful completion
Class Format
•	One-on-one (private) online classes
•	Flexible scheduling — class days and times arranged around the student's availability, including different time zones
•	Separate male and female teachers available — students can choose a tutor they're comfortable with
•	Personalized pace tailored to the student's progress
•	Suitable for both children and adults
•	Class recordings provided on demand, so students can revisit lessons anytime for revision
Free Trial Class
A free trial class is available before enrollment, so students and parents can experience the teaching style and ensure it's the right fit before committing
What Happens After Completion
Upon finishing Noorani Qaida, students are ready to move on to the Quran Reading course, where they'll begin reading directly from the Quran with proper Tajweed application, building on the foundation established here.

Who Is This For
Anyone starting their Quran journey — whether a child learning to read for the first time or an adult revisiting their Quran reading skills from the basics.

Tafseer of the Quran
Understanding the Meanings of Allah's Words
This course takes students through the meanings, context, and lessons of the entire Quran — Surah by Surah — using explanations grounded in the Quran, authentic Hadith, and the understanding of the Salaf (the first three generations of Muslims), free from sectarianism and personal opinion.
Who Is This For
Both kids/teens and adults, offered as separate tracks suited to each age group's level of understanding
Our Approach
Tafseer is taught strictly according to the understanding of the Salaf as-Salih, ensuring students receive an authentic explanation of the Quran as understood by the earliest and best generations of Muslims — not personal interpretation or sectarian bias
Prerequisites
Basic Quran reading ability recommended; no prior Tafseer knowledge required
Module Structure
The whole Quran is divided into 5 modules, each covering 6 Juz. Students may start with any module based on interest or familiarity:
•	Module 1: Juz 25–30 (Juz Amma and surrounding Surahs)
•	Module 2: Juz 19–24
•	Module 3: Juz 13–18
•	Module 4: Juz 7–12
•	Module 5: Juz 1–6
By the End, Students Will Be Able To
•	Understand the meaning and context of Quranic verses covered in their module
•	Explain key lessons and rulings drawn from each Surah
•	Connect Quranic teachings to daily life and character
•	Recognize authentic Tafseer methodology as opposed to personal opinion or sectarian interpretation
Course Duration
Ongoing — progressing module by module at the student's pace
Class Format

Quranic Arabic
Understanding the Language of the Quran
This course equips students with the grammar and vocabulary needed to understand the Quran directly in its original Arabic — moving beyond translation to grasp the depth and precision of Allah's words as revealed.
Who Is This For
Students who already know the Arabic alphabet and have basic reading ability, and want to understand the Quran's grammar, structure, and vocabulary
Prerequisites
Must know the Arabic alphabet and have basic reading ability
Course Structure
The course is divided into 3 levels, each lasting 3 months (9 months total):
•	Level 1 (Foundations): Basic Quranic vocabulary (most frequently occurring words), simple sentence structure, introduction to Nahw (grammar) and Sarf (word structure)
•	Level 2 (Building Understanding): Expanded Quranic vocabulary, deeper grammar rules, understanding verb forms and sentence patterns as used in the Quran
•	Level 3 (Direct Comprehension): Advanced grammar application, word-by-word understanding of Quranic verses, developing the ability to read and comprehend Quranic Arabic directly
By the End, Students Will Be Able To
•	Understand core Quranic grammar (Nahw & Sarf)
•	Recognize and understand high-frequency Quranic vocabulary
•	Read Quranic verses and grasp their direct meaning without relying solely on translation
•	Build a strong foundation for deeper Tafseer and Quranic studies

Quran Memorization (Hifz)
Preserving the Words of Allah in the Heart
This course guides students through memorizing the Quran using a structured, time-tested system of daily lessons and revision — helping students not just memorize, but retain and preserve what they've learned for life.
Who Is This For
Students who can already read the Quran and are ready to begin the journey of memorization, at any age
Prerequisites
Basic Quran reading ability (completion of Noorani Qaida or equivalent)
Memorization System
Progress follows the traditional Sabaq, Sabqi, and Manzil system:
•	Sabaq — new daily memorization portion
•	Sabqi — recent revision, reinforcing recently memorized portions
•	Manzil — long-term revision, regularly reviewing previously memorized sections of the Quran to ensure lifelong retention
Pace
Flexible and personalized — each student progresses according to their own ability and consistency, without being rushed or held to a rigid schedule
By the End, Students Will Be Able To
•	Recite memorized portions of the Quran fluently and accurately
•	Apply proper Tajweed while reciting from memory
•	Retain memorized portions long-term through consistent revision
•	Build a lifelong habit of Quran review and preservation
Course Duration
Ongoing — no fixed duration; students may pursue partial memorization (select Surahs/Juz) or work toward completing the full Quran
Class Format
•	One-on-one (private) online classes
•	Flexible scheduling — class days and times arranged around the student's availability
•	Both male and female teachers available — students/parents can choose the tutor they're most comfortable with
•	Class recordings provided on demand, so lessons can be revisited anytime
•	Suitable for both children and adults
Assessment
Regular progress assessments, with progress reports shared with parents for younger students, tracking both new memorization and revision retention
Free Trial Class
A free trial class is available before enrollment, so families can experience the teaching style and ensure it's the right fit
What Happens After Completion
Students who complete the full Quran memorization may pursue an Ijazah (certified chain of transmission) or continue focusing on Tajweed refinement and lifelong revision.
