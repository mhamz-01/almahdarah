# 1. Tafsir of the Qur'an

### Short Description

Study the meanings of the Noble Qur'an through the explanations of the righteous predecessors (Salaf) and authentic narrations, learning how to understand Allah's guidance and apply it in everyday life.

### Full Description

The Qur'an is the ultimate source of guidance for every Muslim. This Tafsir course is designed to help students understand the meanings of the Qur'an through the explanations of the righteous predecessors (Salaf), the authentic Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, and the works of reliable scholars of Ahl al-Sunnah.

Rather than limiting the study to translation alone, this course explores the context of verses, their lessons, and the wisdom behind Allah's commands and prohibitions. Students will learn how the Qur'an shapes one's beliefs, character, worship, family life, and interactions with society.

Throughout the course, emphasis is placed on reflecting upon the Qur'an so that its teachings become a practical guide for daily life. By understanding Allah's words correctly, students strengthen their relationship with the Qur'an, increase their īmān (faith), and learn to approach life's challenges through the guidance revealed by Allah.

Whether you are beginning your journey of seeking knowledge or wish to deepen your understanding of the Qur'an, this course provides a structured and authentic foundation for lifelong learning.

**Class Schedule:** Every **Monday at 7:50 PM**

# 2. Umdat al-Ahkam (Hadith)

### Short Description

Study authentic hadiths related to worship and daily life, learning the rulings and practical Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ regarding prayer, fasting, zakah, Hajj, purification, and more.

### Full Description

'Umdat al-Ahkam is among the most beneficial collections of authentic hadiths focusing on Islamic rulings (Ahkam). Compiled primarily from Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim, it serves as an excellent introduction to understanding practical Islam directly from the authentic Sunnah.

In this course, students study the authentic narrations of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ concerning purification, prayer (Salah), fasting (Sawm), zakah, Hajj, marriage, business dealings, manners, and many other aspects of everyday life. Alongside the text, the explanations clarify how these hadiths are understood and applied according to the methodology of the scholars of Ahl al-Sunnah.

The aim is not only to memorize narrations but to understand how every act of worship and daily practice should be performed according to the authentic Sunnah. Students gain confidence in practicing Islam upon authentic evidence while developing a greater love and appreciation for the teachings of Allah's Messenger ﷺ.

This course is suitable for anyone seeking to strengthen their worship and align their daily life with the authentic guidance of the Prophet ﷺ.

**Class Schedule:** Every **Thursday at 7:50 PM**

# 3. Arabic Language (Ad-Dā' wa ad-Dawā')

### Short Description

Develop the ability to understand classical Arabic through practical reading of authentic Islamic texts while gradually building vocabulary, grammar, and reading skills.

### Full Description

Understanding the Arabic language opens the door to directly benefiting from the Qur'an, the Sunnah, and the rich heritage of classical Islamic scholarship. This course adopts a practical, application-based approach to learning Arabic rather than relying solely on theoretical grammar.

Students study classical Arabic through authentic texts, including *Ad-Dā' wa ad-Dawā'* by Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah (رحمه الله), learning how to read, analyze, and understand original Arabic works with increasing confidence.

Alongside reading practice, students steadily expand their Arabic vocabulary, improve sentence comprehension, and strengthen their understanding of Arabic grammar. Additional foundational texts such as *Al-Ajurrumiyyah* may also be used to explain essential grammatical principles whenever needed.

By the end of the course, students develop the skills required to approach classical Arabic books independently, making future studies of Tafsir, Hadith, Aqeedah, and Fiqh more accessible.

This course is ideal for anyone who wishes to move beyond translations and engage directly with the language of the Qur'an and classical Islamic scholarship.

# 4. Aqeedah al-Wasitiyyah

### Short Description

Study the fundamental beliefs of Islam according to the Qur'an, the authentic Sunnah, and the understanding of the righteous predecessors through one of the most renowned classical texts on Islamic creed.

### Full Description

Correct belief ('Aqeedah) is the foundation upon which every act of worship is built. This course studies *Al-'Aqeedah al-Wasitiyyah*, the famous work of Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah (رحمه الله), which concisely explains the creed of Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah based upon the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah.

Students learn the essential principles of Islamic belief, including faith in Allah, His Names and Attributes, the Angels, the Divine Books, the Messengers, the Last Day, Divine Decree (Qadr), and other core matters of faith as understood by the righteous predecessors (Salaf).

The course also clarifies common misconceptions, strengthens certainty in one's beliefs, and provides students with a clear understanding of the foundations upon which Islam is established. Every topic is presented with textual evidence from the Qur'an and authentic hadith, together with the explanations of the early scholars.

A sound understanding of Aqeedah increases certainty, sincerity in worship, and helps every Muslim worship Allah upon correct knowledge and clear guidance.
